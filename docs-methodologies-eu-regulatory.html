EU regulatory

A pluggable regional compliance adapter, built around the EU Waste Shipment Regulation (EU) 2024/1157 and the DIWASS digital exchange system it establishes.

26 rulesInternal only

These 26 rules are ImpacTrakr's own implementation of EU waste-shipment and waste-framework requirements as they apply to the jurisdiction a project operates in today. That's an important distinction to hold onto: this is the platform's engineering interpretation of the regulation, not a certified or legally-reviewed compliance statement.

The adapter is deliberately built to be regional and swappable — the pattern exists so a different country's rule set can plug in later without re-architecting the validation engine, rather than hard-coding one jurisdiction's rules into the core.

Per the platform's rule-governance decision (see Standard), EU regulatory verdicts stay internal: they're validated and recorded in full, and surfaced in the consolidated homologation/evidence document, but they are not part of any external registry submission payload.
