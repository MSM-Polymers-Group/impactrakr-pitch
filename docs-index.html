dMRV Multi-tenant SaaS Registry-agnostic

ImpacTrakr is a digital MRV (Monitoring, Reporting, Verification) platform. It turns a recycler's operational data — weights, chain of custody, permits, lab results — into an auditable record of a tracked batch of material (a MassID), validated against a project's rule sets before anything is ever submitted anywhere external.

One thing worth being upfront about: unlike some of the registries ImpacTrakr can feed data into, ImpacTrakr itself is not a public protocol with its own independent governance or foundation — it's a vendor platform, built and operated by one team, that a client organization runs their own tenant on.
